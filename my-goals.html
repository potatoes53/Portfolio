My Goals in Life

  • Goal 1

    I'm a health-oriented guy. I want to take care of my body so that I can live an active life, even as I grow old. My body is me, and if I don't take care of it, I will become weak. This means I will have keep to my diet, inform myself on the chemicals that are ever prevalent in today's world, and get exercise. These are things that I am doing right now; however, the key is consistency.

    In ancient Greece, there's a word describing the ultimate form one can wish to achieve, a balance between mind and muscle: Arête.
    This is what I strive for.

    (>-----<>-----<)

  • Goal 2

    Questing for information is something that I want to improve more and more. Information is power and it seems like it is becoming cheaper with every passing day. Many of us waste our precious time with these magical devices in our pockets and on our desks watching trivial videos and consuming pointless news articles. I am no exception. I play video games for hours, watch plenty of short-form videos, and read pointless news articles. I don't like it. I want to change.

    My 2nd goal is to make my virtuous quest for knowledge enjoyable, so I don't go looking for brain rot content. Did you know brain rot is a word in the Oxford Dictionary?

    The many luminaries and geniuses of our history would surely revel in the opportunity to speak to a demi-god of information and hear a reply. What a widely unremarked privilege it is to have access to these wonderful machines.

    (>-----<>-----<)

  • Goal 3

    Friendship. Friendship is something that is very valuable to me. I've always had trouble making friends, however. I want to learn how to create networks of friends so that I can learn and grow as a person. I've heard that the quality of a persons life largely depends on their social life. As a person who has few friends, and fewer friends that I actually see in person, I think this is very true. Although it is a simple goal on the surface, I think tackling goal will be tough for me. Nonetheless, it is important to me.

    My third goal is to learn how to create and sustain healthy relationships.
