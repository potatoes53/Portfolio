About Me

I was just the run-of-the-mill, bored school student. I didn't like class, I looked for excuses to stay home, and I watched the clock impatiently as it ticked away the seconds, minutes, and hours.

Everyday was the same to me. I'd wake up, eat some sugary cereal early in the morning, and spend the majority of day-light hours sitting statue like in a classroom, begging for something INTERESTING to happen. I was completely unenthusiastic about the work I was doing. All I could think of all day was getting home and spending the rest of my dwindling hours left playing video games and watching youtube.

This process went on for years. Years of dispair and disinterest and imagination-squashing and joy-killing and systematic enstupification. It was killing me.

Then, FINALLY, after the culmination of light mental disease, vague school social ostracization, and existential befuddlement, my parents caved and pulled me out of the coal mine. I was finally free--or so I thought. It turns out that after leaving 'regular' school, you have online school, which I was subsequently FORCED into (hooray).

Here's the issue: When you take a person out of a system that tell them precisely what to do, they're brain's gonna be fucking confused (oh my, a cuss!).

It was maybe a change for the worse. Now I was procrastinating, not getting ANY social interaction, and still feeling very existentially stranded.

Now, even though I like to look back on those days and say, "wow, that was a shit show!" (goodness gracious), I did grow a lot during that time. I watched videos on philosophy and science, listened to great speakers in various fields, and started to develop some pretty rad 3D modeling skills.

But it wasn't enough, of course. I was very much failing academically and NONE of my efforts were being recorded whatsoever. But that's when my Mom found out about !!!!!!

In a time of confusion and little hope, Pacific Sands Academy served as a beacon of hope that I could cling onto. Finally all the work I was doing could be recorded and acknowledged as more than just the shenanigans of a unschooled-kid. Shauna, the Founder and my advisor , has guided and supported me during my time learning and exploring the world thus far, and with her help, I have been empowered to be couragous and creative. Instead of the endless streams of worksheets and homework, I follow my passion and learn out of genuine interest.

I am very grateful to be a part of Pacific Sands Academy, and I am excited to see where I go from here.
Thank you, Mrs. Shauna, PSA.
